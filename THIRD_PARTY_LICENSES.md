# Third-party licenses

## Fonts

This release does not bundle or redistribute third-party font binaries.

The Android font picker exposes forty-two print presets derived from generic Android system
font families (`serif`, `sans-serif`, `cursive`, and installed variants). These fonts are
provided by the device operating system and are not copied into the APK. Style names such
as “楷体”, “行楷” and “隶书” describe the app's family/weight/scale/skew presets; they do not
claim to redistribute commercial fonts with those names.

Future bundled fonts must be recorded here with the font name, source URL, exact license,
copyright notice, and redistribution terms before their files are added to the project.

Fonts selected through “我的字体” are supplied by the user at runtime, copied only into that
installation's private `filesDir/fonts` directory, and are never bundled with or redistributed
in the APK. The user is responsible for confirming that their imported font license permits the
intended preview and printing use.

## Footer floral decorations

All footer flowers and memorial ornaments in this release are original procedural Android
Canvas paths drawn by the app. No third-party PNG, SVG, screenshot, or commercial artwork is
bundled, so there is no external artwork license or attribution requirement.

## Border ornaments

All border templates are original procedural Android Canvas paths based on generic traditional
geometric concepts such as key, cloud, wave, bead, diamond, and leaf patterns. No third-party
border image, SVG, screenshot, or commercial artwork is bundled.
